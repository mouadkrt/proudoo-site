Kids' routines that stick, one streak at a time

Morning, bedtime, homework: Proudoo turns your child's routines into daily quests and a streak they're proud to keep. Because “I'm someone who does this” lasts longer than any reminder.

Routines as daily quests

Each step of a routine can be a quest: brush teeth, get dressed, pack the school bag, read for 20 minutes. Daily quests come back every day; weekly ones once a week. Your child sees exactly what's left for today and ticks it off on their own.

Streaks are about identity, not counting

Every day your child completes their quests, their streak grows. After a week, it's not just a number anymore: it's proof they're the kind of kid who shows up. Milestones at 3, 7 and 30 days come with a little celebration.

A missed day doesn't erase everything

Life happens: illness, a trip, a busy weekend. Your child can buy a streak freeze with the coins they earned, and it protects their streak on a missed day. If the streak does break, a same-day repair, also paid in coins, can bring it back. Never with real money. You set the prices, or turn these off.

A gentle reminder at the right time

Choose when the daily reminder goes out, for example at 7 pm. It's only sent if a quest is still due and nothing has been submitted yet, and you can switch it off entirely.

Routine ideas to start with

Morning

  • Make the bed
  • Get dressed on my own
  • Brush my teeth
  • Put my breakfast bowl in the sink

After school and evening

  • Unpack my school bag
  • Homework or 20 minutes of reading
  • Pack my bag for tomorrow
  • Pajamas and teeth before story time

Tip: start with just one step of one routine. When it's automatic, add the next.
