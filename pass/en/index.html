How to get it

  1. Note the email of your Proudoo parent account (the one you sign in with).
  2. Message us on WhatsApp with: "Family Pass, monthly" or "yearly", and that email.
  3. We send you the bank details for the transfer.
  4. Send us the transfer receipt in the same chat.
  5. Your family is activated within 24 hours. Close and reopen the app: the new features appear.

Questions

What happens when the pass ends?

Your family goes back to the free version. Nothing is deleted: the child bank, children beyond two and older history are hidden, and come back as soon as you take a pass again. We email you a few days before.

What if I change my mind?

You have 7 days after activation to ask for a full refund, no reason needed. Message us on WhatsApp or write to support@proudoo.app.

Paying from abroad?

Write to us: we'll find the best way.
