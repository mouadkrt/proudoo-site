Pocket money and rewards your child chooses

In Proudoo, children earn coins by completing quests and spend them on rewards they pick themselves. It's a gentle first lesson in effort, saving and choice, without real money changing hands in the app.

Coins first, then choices

Every quest has a coin value that you set. When you approve a finished quest, the coins land in your child's balance. They can spend them right away on a small reward, or save up for something bigger. Either way, the choice is theirs.

Why the child chooses the reward

A reward imposed by an adult is a transaction. A reward a child picks is a goal. You set a few options and their prices; your child decides what's worth working for. They can also send you wishes: ideas for new rewards you can accept, adjust or decline.

Reward ideas that aren't just treats

Time and moments

  • Choose what's for dinner on Friday
  • An extra bedtime story
  • A board game with mom or dad
  • A trip to the park or the pool
  • Pick the family movie

Privileges and bigger goals

  • Stay up 30 minutes later on the weekend
  • Invite a friend over
  • A book or a small toy they've been wanting
  • A day out they plan themselves
  • Coins turned into real pocket money, at a rate you choose

Keeping track of real pocket money

If your child also gets real pocket money, Proudoo's family bank lets you record it: deposits, withdrawals and a running balance your child can see. It's a ledger you fill in, not a bank account. No money moves through the app and no card is needed. The family bank is one of the extra features for parents.

A few tips from families

  • Keep prices fair. A first reward should be reachable within a few days.
  • Mix small and big rewards, so there's always something close and something to save for.
  • Gift coins for effort now and then, with a reason: “for helping your sister without being asked.”
  • Don't pay for everything. Some things are just part of family life. Quests are for the habits you're building.
