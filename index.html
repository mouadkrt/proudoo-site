The real goal isn't a spotless house

Most chore charts end up on the fridge, then in a drawer. Reminders turn into nagging, and the chore becomes a fight. Proudoo starts from a different idea: a child who feels proud of what they did, and a parent who feels relief. The chores are just the way there.

So the app is built around the moments that matter: the quest your child finished on their own, the streak they kept on a busy day, the reward they picked themselves, and the “I'm proud of you” you send with one tap.

Where does the direction come from?

At first, from you. One day, from them. The reason for doing something moves, step by step, from the outside to the inside.

Backed by research

Self-determination theory, by psychologists Edward Deci and Richard Ryan, has been studied since the 1970s and is one of the most tested theories of human motivation. The four stages above are its own: external, introjected, identified and integrated motivation.

How it works

  1. Set up your family in minutes. Add your children, create a few quests (daily, weekly or one-time) and give each one a coin value.
  2. Your child completes quests. They tick a quest when it's done, with a photo if they like. Coins, XP and their streak grow, and their character evolves as they level up.
  3. You approve and celebrate. One inbox to approve or send back a quest with a kind note. Your child spends their coins on a reward they choose.

Built on a few simple ideas

  • Fair, always. The rules are clear and the app never feels unfair to a child. When in doubt, it sides with them.
  • Your child chooses the reward. You set the options and prices; they pick. Ownership is what keeps motivation alive.
  • Streaks are about identity. “I'm someone who makes my bed” lasts longer than a sticker.
  • Start embarrassingly small. One quest a day is enough. Momentum first, ambition later.
  • Growth, not a task list. Each quest can count toward body, mind, heart, family bonds, skills and character, and reports show how your child is growing.

Made for families, safe for kids

  • No ads, ever, and no selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No email or phone number for children. They join with a code from you, then sign in with a 4-digit PIN.
  • Shared devices welcome. Several children can use the same phone or tablet.
  • You stay in control. Photo proof and the AI photo check are optional, and you can delete your family's data from the app at any time. Read the privacy policy.

On iPhone? Use Proudoo as a web app

Proudoo on iPhone

Proudoo isn't on the App Store yet, but it works on iPhone as a web app:

  1. Open web.proudoo.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

Proudoo then opens full-screen with its own icon, like any app. For now there are no notifications on iPhone.
