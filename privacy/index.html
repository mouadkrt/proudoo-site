1. Who We Are

Proudoo ("we", "us", "our") is a family chore app published on Google Play. It is operated by Sentissi Mouad, an individual developer based in Morocco, who is responsible for the personal data processed through the app. The app is offered in Morocco and Canada.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: Sentissi Mouad, operator of Proudoo. For any privacy question or request, contact: support@proudoo.app

2. What This App Does

Proudoo is a gamified chore app for families. Parents create a family account, set up quests (chores) and rewards for their children, and children complete quests to earn XP, coins, streaks and rewards. The app is designed to be used by parents and their minor children under direct parental supervision.

3. Information We Collect

From Parents

  • Email address and password (handled by our sign-in provider, Clerk; we never see your password)
  • Display name
  • Family name and settings, including whether the optional AI photo check is turned on
  • The quests, rewards, notes and messages you create (for example "proud of you" messages)

About Children (entered by the parent or created by using the app)

  • First name and birth year, entered by the parent
  • A 4-digit PIN, stored only in hashed form
  • Quest completions and their history, XP, level, coins, streaks and avatar
  • Rewards, wishes and the coin bank (a ledger the parent fills in; no real money moves through the app)
  • Photos a child takes as proof that a quest is done (optional, see section 4)
  • If the parent turned on the AI photo check: the AI's score, label and short feedback for each photo (see section 5)

We do not collect a child's email address, phone number, location or advertising ID. Children do not have their own sign-in account; they join the family with a code and PIN set up by the parent.

Automatically Collected

  • Push notification tokens for the devices you and your children use, so the app can send notifications
  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • Crash and error reports (see Sentry in section 7)
  • Server logs, which include IP addresses, kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting

We do not collect location data, contacts or microphone data, and we do not use the advertising ID. The camera (and, where enabled, the photo gallery) is used only when a child chooses to attach a photo to a quest.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate and identify family accounts
  • Show quests, progress, streaks, XP, coins and rewards to the right family
  • Let parents review and approve completed quests, including any photo proof
  • Run the optional AI photo check, when the parent turns it on (section 5)
  • Send notifications (for example a quest submitted or approved, a "proud of you" message, a streak reminder)
  • Keep the app working, find and fix errors
  • Respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing or profiling, and we have no ads or analytics tracking in the app.

5. Optional AI Photo Check

Parents can turn on an AI photo check in Settings. It is off by default, and a notice explaining what is sent is shown before it can be turned on.

  • What is sent: when a child submits a quest with a photo, the photo and the quest's title and description are sent to Google Cloud's AI service (Vertex AI, Gemini). The child's name, birth year and account details are not sent.
  • What comes back: a score, a label ("looks complete", "uncertain" or "looks incomplete") and one short sentence of feedback for the parent. This is stored with the quest completion.
  • Who decides: the result is advice for the parent, who makes the final decision. If the parent also turns on auto-approve, quests whose score reaches the threshold the parent chose are approved automatically; the activity feed then shows they were approved by the AI, and the parent can turn this off at any time.
  • How Google handles it: Google processes this data as our service provider under its Google Cloud terms. Google does not use it to train its AI models. Google may keep it in memory for up to 24 hours to speed up processing, and may log requests to detect abuse of its service, as its terms allow.
  • Turning it off: parents can turn the AI photo check off at any time in Settings. Photos are then no longer sent.

6. Children's Privacy

Proudoo is designed for use by families, including young children, under direct parental supervision and control. Parents create and manage the family account. Children do not create accounts on their own or provide contact information.

  • A child's information is collected only with the consent of the parent, who sets up each child's profile (for children under 14, consent always comes from the parent)
  • Photos are only taken when a child chooses to attach one. In the app, only the child's own family can see them; our staff can open one only for support or safety reasons, and every such access is logged
  • Children's photos are sent to the AI service only if the parent has turned the AI photo check on (section 5)
  • Parents can review and change their child's data at any time in the app. To delete one child, go to Settings → Family & Members → Delete next to that child: the profile and all of that child's data are erased immediately and permanently. You can also contact us at support@proudoo.app.
  • We do not share children's data with third parties for advertising or marketing

8. Data Security

We protect your data using:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Access-controlled database and storage; quest photos are private, served only through our app, and staff access to them is logged
  • Hashed child PINs, and parent sign-in managed by Clerk

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We are committed to using commercially reasonable means to protect your information.

9. Data Retention

  • Quest photos are deleted automatically 30 days after the parent reviews the quest, or 30 days after it was submitted if it was never reviewed.
  • Completed quests (status, parent notes, AI photo check results) are deleted after 12 months.
  • Activity history: after 12 months, the details (quest titles, notes) are erased; only the type and date of each activity are kept, for statistics.
  • Notification tokens are deleted after 60 days without the app being opened on that device.
  • Crash reports are deleted by Sentry after 30 days; server logs after 30 days.
  • Everything else (account, child profiles, rewards, coin bank) is kept as long as the account is in use.
  • Inactive accounts: if nobody in a family uses Proudoo for 24 months, we email every parent in the family. If the account is still not used 30 days later, the family and all its data are deleted. Opening the app is enough to keep it.
  • When an account is deleted, its data is deleted straight away (see section 10). Any short-term backups held by our hosting providers expire on their normal schedule.

10. Account Deletion

You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data in two ways:

  1. In-app: Go to Settings → Delete Account
  2. By email: Send a deletion request to support@proudoo.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" (details: proudoo.app/delete-account)

To remove only one child and keep the rest of the family, use Settings → Family & Members → Delete next to that child. The app first shows exactly what will be erased, and you confirm by typing the child's name.

If you are the only parent in your family, deletion removes the whole family: parent and child profiles, quest history, photos, rewards and notification tokens. If another parent shares your family, only your own account is removed and the family stays with the other parent.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you or your child
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example by turning off the AI photo check

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@proudoo.app. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact your data protection authority:

  • Morocco: Commission nationale de contrôle de la protection des données à caractère personnel (CNDP), cndp.ma
  • Quebec: Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, cai.gouv.qc.ca
  • Elsewhere in Canada: Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, priv.gc.ca
