Questions from parents

Everything families ask us before getting started. Can't find your answer? Email support@proudoo.app.

What age is Proudoo for?

Proudoo is designed for children aged 6 to 14. Younger children may need a parent nearby to read their quests; older children can manage their quests on their own.

Is Proudoo available on iPhone?

Yes, as a web app. Open web.proudoo.app in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Proudoo then opens full-screen with its own icon, like any app. A few things differ from Android: there are no notifications on iPhone for now, and a quest photo can come from the photo library as well as the camera. On Android, Proudoo is on Google Play.

Is Proudoo free?

Proudoo is free to download, with no ads. The heart of the app is free: quests, coins, levels, streaks, the reward store and “proud of you”. A few extra features for parents are paid.

Does my child need an email address or a phone?

No. Children don't have an account with an email or phone number. You give your child a join code from the app, and they choose a 4-digit PIN to sign in.

Can several children share one tablet?

Yes. Several children can be signed in on the same phone or tablet, each with their own profile, quests and coins.

Can both parents manage the family?

Yes. You can invite a co-parent, who can create quests, approve them and send “proud of you” too.

Do photos and the AI check have to be used?

No. Attaching a photo is optional for the child. The AI photo check is off by default; if a parent turns it on, the photo and the quest title are sent to Google's AI service to suggest a score before the parent decides. Photos are deleted 30 days after review.

What happens if my child misses a day?

Their streak resets, unless they've bought a streak freeze with their coins. If it breaks, a same-day repair, also paid with coins, can bring it back. Real money is never involved.

Is the family bank a real bank account?

No. It's a ledger where you record your child's real pocket money (deposits and withdrawals) so they can see their balance. No money moves through the app.

Which languages are available?

English and French. You can switch language inside the app.

How do I delete my data?

From the app: Settings, then delete your account. Your family's data is erased. You can also email support@proudoo.app; see the account deletion page for details.

More on privacy in the privacy policy and on deleting your account.
