Terms of sale

Last Updated: November 13, 2026 · Version française (the French version prevails)

1. Seller

The Family Pass is sold by the publisher of the Proudoo app, an independent developer based in Morocco. Contact: support@proudoo.app.

2. What is sold

The Family Pass gives one Proudoo family (all its parents and children) access to the features described on the Family Pass page, for the period bought. Free features stay available without a pass. The list of included features may change; a feature included at purchase stays included until the end of the paid period.

3. Price and payment

  • 25 MAD for one month, or 250 MAD for one year, in Moroccan dirhams.
  • Payment by bank transfer. Bank details are sent on request, by WhatsApp or email.
  • Any fees charged by your bank are yours.
  • The price that applies is the one shown on the Family Pass page on the day of the request.

4. Activation and duration

  • The pass is activated on the family whose parent email you give us, within 24 hours of receiving the transfer receipt.
  • The period (one month or one year) starts at activation. If the family already had a trial or a pass running, the new period is added after it.
  • No automatic renewal. When the period ends, the family goes back to the free version. We email you a few days before.
  • No data is deleted when a pass ends: pass features are hidden, and come back with a new pass.

5. Free trial

Every new family gets every feature once, for 14 days, with no payment or commitment.

6. Refunds

You can ask for a full refund of your pass within 7 days of activation, no reason needed, by WhatsApp or at support@proudoo.app. The refund is made by bank transfer within 15 days, and the pass is removed.

7. Availability

We do our best to keep Proudoo running at all times but cannot guarantee it. If Proudoo is down for more than 7 days in a row through our fault, your pass is extended by the same length, on request.

8. Personal data

The information needed for payment is handled under our privacy policy ("Family Pass" section).
